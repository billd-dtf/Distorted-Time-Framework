\begin{abstract}\noindent
We take the rate of time as primitive: how fast a clock ticks, measured against its neighbours, is a physical
fact, not a consequence of geometry. There is no global ``now'', and geometry follows from the rate.

One move, three consequences. Gravitational time dilation and quantum phase are \emph{one} accumulated
quantity: a redshift and an interference fringe measure the same clock. Gravitational and inertial mass are
equal because there is only one rate. And local nows stitch into one cosmic time only if the cosmic flow has
no twist; ours has almost none, which is a fact about how the universe began rather than anything the
expansion produced, so cosmic time is an excellent approximation and not an exact fact.

Theories of this kind fail in two ways: those that make time global die on that global object, and those that
keep it local must add one back to recover cosmology and entanglement. DTF needs neither. We start where the
Wheeler--DeWitt equation does, with no time, and let time build from the local rates, so entanglement stays on
that timeless layer rather than on a spatial slice. Realist quantum mechanics needs no preferred frame, and
the correlations remain non-signalling.

This is an organizing-principle claim in the sense of Laughlin and Pines~\cite{LaughlinPines2000}: the content
is the reorganization, not a new number. Where general relativity and quantum mechanics have been tested DTF
agrees with both, and only the assumption count changes: the equivalence principle and cosmic simultaneity
stop being separate postulates. It stakes one falsifiable claim, a horizonless, echo-free black-hole remnant
replacing the singularity. Kill it and the reframe is dead.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction --- why this ontology}\label{sec:intro}

One commitment organizes this paper. The local rate at which a clock ticks is proper time, read from each
point to its neighbours, and we treat it as a physical primitive rather than a gauge coordinate. Making it primitive does not
relabel general relativity; it \emph{forces} connections the separate tellings of gravity and quantum
mechanics have no reason to draw. The sharpest: a particle's quantum phase accumulates at its internal
frequency $\omega_C$ over proper time, and gravitational redshift is the variation of that same proper-time
rate $u$, the lapse. Both run through one lapse,
\[ \Phi=\omega_C\!\int u\,dt , \]
so time dilation and phase accumulation are two readings of a single accumulated $\Phi$, though general
relativity and quantum mechanics keep them in separate chapters. A reader who sees an accumulated phase and
thinks of Fermat's principle is reading it correctly: making $\Phi$ stationary gives classical motion and
summing over it gives quantum motion, which is the one variational rule used throughout (\S\ref{sec:rate}, \S\ref{sec:quantum}). That mass \emph{is} a frequency $\omega_C$ is de~Broglie's
clock~\cite{deBroglie1925}, now measured~\cite{Lan2013} and formalised~\cite{Dolce2011}; the new step is to make
the \emph{lapse} $u$ primitive --- \emph{not} to call it $\omega_C$, but to see that this one geometric rate
both redshifts every clock and clocks every phase.

\paragraph{The moves, stated plainly.} We do not ask the reader to uncover the commitments; here they are, each
made good in the section named.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt,label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Three rows, each emerging from the one before.}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0pt,topsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{Row 0} --- the timeless substrate ($\hat H\psi=0$~\cite{DeWitt1967}, no external
    time~\cite{Isham1993}): the ledger of entanglement and conservation.
  \item \emph{Row 1} --- the local clock-rate $u$ emerges: time, energy, and the clock's tick-count $N$
    whose logarithm is the entropy; also gravity, inertia, guidance.
  \item \emph{Row 2} --- space emerges as the rate's radiation: distance, the two gravitational-wave
    polarisations, $\hbar$, and coarse-grained \emph{statistical} thermodynamics (temperature, heat, Hawking
    radiation) --- emergent, never fundamental.
  \end{itemize}
  \noindent (\emph{Entropy} is not divided by this. There is one entropy, $S=\ln N$, the ordinary logarithm
  of the number of distinguishable states. The rows answer a prior question, \emph{what makes states distinguishable}, and at Row 1 the answer is the clock. Heat is a Row-2 way of
  reaching a large $N$, not a second kind of entropy, so no Row-0/Row-1 derivation here may use a thermal
  quantity. Made good in \S\ref{sec:rate} and Appendix~G.)
\item \textbf{Energy is a frequency:} $E=\hbar\omega_C$ --- mass is an internal frequency, which the lapse $u$
  modulates ($u$ is the primitive, not $\omega_C$) (\S\ref{sec:rate}).
\item \textbf{Space is time's radiation:} the radiating sector is the spatial metric's transverse-traceless
  part (two polarisations); the clock-rate itself does not radiate (\S\ref{sec:gravity}).
\item \textbf{The now is local:} no global slice; a shared cosmic time emerges only as a limit. Entanglement is a Row-0 phenomenon that cannot signal (\S\ref{sec:quantum}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What these recover.} From these blocks the standard results follow as \emph{consequences, not
inputs}: the equivalence principle (\S\ref{sec:equiv}); Newtonian and weak-field gravity, and Kerr (\S\ref{sec:equiv}); the two
gravitational-wave modes with $\alpha_1=\alpha_2=0$ (\S\ref{sec:gravity}); Schr\"odinger's equation and the Born rule (\S\ref{sec:quantum});
and the Tsirelson bound (\S\ref{sec:quantum}). In every tested regime the framework is general relativity plus quantum
mechanics.

\paragraph{What these unify, dissolve, and predict.} Beyond recovery, the one primitive does three things GR
and QM, told separately, do not:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Unify} --- gravitational redshift $=$ quantum phase (the shared $\Phi$); gravity and inertia (one
  rate, \S\ref{sec:equiv}); measurement and the black-hole core as one clock-definiteness surface (\S\ref{sec:quantum},\S\ref{sec:consistency}); the galactic
  scale $a_0$ tied to the dark-energy horizon (\S\ref{sec:consistency}).
\item \textbf{Dissolve} --- three questions stop being askable rather than being answered: why gravitational and
  inertial mass match (\S\ref{sec:equiv}); why realist QM needs a preferred frame (it does not, since entanglement lives on the timeless Row~0, \S\ref{sec:quantum}); and the black-hole information paradox (no singularity destroys information; it is held
  in Row~0, \S\ref{sec:consistency}).
\item \textbf{Predict} --- a horizonless, echo-free black-hole remnant (\S\ref{sec:consistency}): the one falsifiable owe; and,
  at plausibility level, the \emph{value} $a_0\approx cH_0/2\pi$ MOND leaves as a free constant (\S\ref{sec:consistency}). The
  decaying frame-dragging residual (\S\ref{sec:rate}) is \emph{not} offered as a test: it is unobservable by fifty
  orders of magnitude.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What we then show.} The body makes each move good and consistent with existing mathematics and
experiment, and is honest about the one place DTF commits to something new that could be wrong: a horizonless,
echo-free black-hole remnant in place of the singularity (\S\ref{sec:consistency}). \textbf{Kill it and the reframe is dead.} This is an organizing-principle claim in the sense of Laughlin
and Pines~\cite{LaughlinPines2000} --- its value is the reorganization, not a new number; the posit-count is
quantified in Appendix~F.

\paragraph{Why this set of moves, and not the neighbours'.} The time-primitive neighbourhood is a graveyard where global-time-only and local-time-only theories all have well-known failure points. DTF solves for each of these issues.

\emph{Global time first.} Neo-Lorentzian frames, dynamical clock fields (Einstein-\ae ther, khronometric,
Ho\v{r}ava~\cite{Jacobson2001,BlasPujolasSibiryakov2011,Horava2009}), varying-$c$, and preferred-foliation
quantum theories~\cite{DGMZ1999} each die on a \emph{global} time.

\emph{Local time first.} Programmes that reject a background time recover the local fact only by
\emph{adding} a global object (a clock-subsystem correlation~\cite{PageWootters1983}, modular
flow~\cite{ConnesRovelli1994}, or a preferred foliation~\cite{Barbour2002}). If they stay purely
epistemic~\cite{Rovelli1996}, they add no physics.

\emph{DTF escapes both.} It is local, so it never owns the global object the first group dies
on. And its rate is a real physical relation, not a bookkeeping convention: it is how fast this clock runs
against its neighbour, which is what a redshift measurement reads. That is why it need
not \emph{add} a global structure the way the second group does: a shared cosmic time comes back
as a limiting case, valid wherever the vorticity is negligible, as it is in our universe (\S\ref{sec:rate}).

\paragraph{Organization.} \S\ref{sec:rate} states the primitive and the removal of global structure; \S\ref{sec:equiv}--\S\ref{sec:quantum} draw the
equivalence, gravity, and quantum consequences; \S\ref{sec:consistency} collects the remaining owe alongside the agreement with GR and
QM. The Technical Appendix holds the detailed recoveries and the economy ledger (Appendix~F); scripts back
every \emph{verified} result (Code Availability).

\section{The local rate and the removal of global structure}\label{sec:rate}
\worksin{Row 0 $\to$ Row 1 (Wheeler--DeWitt sector $\to$ lapse sector)}

Physics is usually built on space, time a coordinate. DTF wagers the opposite: the local rate at which clocks
run (the ADM lapse) is a physical primitive, emerging from Row 0 (the Wheeler--DeWitt sector, $\hat H\psi=0$,
no time). The primitive is \emph{relational}: how fast a clock ticks relative to its neighbours, chained point to
point. Three consequences follow.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{No global slice.} Each comparison needs only a local rest frame, and the choice is
  non-integrable, so no global $u$ exists. That absence \emph{is} the absence of a preferred foliation.
\item \textbf{Gravity is GR's.} The rate does not propagate ($\pi_u\equiv0$, standard ADM) and the dynamics
  stay Einstein's, so gravity, the two wave modes, and $\alpha_1=\alpha_2=0$ are inherited. No
  gravitational-sector discriminator --- the price of adding nothing.
\item \textbf{The drive.} Covariantly $u$ is the proper-time rate of the matter congruence $n^\mu$, whose
  four-acceleration is the drive, $a^\mu=\nabla^\mu\ln u$ (\S\ref{sec:equiv}).
\end{itemize}

The non-integrability is concrete: the vorticity of $n^\mu$ --- the twist of the worldline congruence,
the quantity frame-dragging measures --- which by Frobenius forbids a global time function
--- and, for a given congruence, \emph{is} the non-integrability measured as frame-dragging ($g_{0i}\neq0$;
GPB~\cite{GravityProbeB2011}, LARES~\cite{LARES2016}). So a \emph{global} now is a theorem, not a posit: it
exists \emph{iff} the vorticity vanishes. One might expect the expansion to supply that limit, since
\[ \omega\sim a^{-2}, \qquad \omega/H\to0 , \]
but the work it does is small: the smallness of cosmic vorticity is an \emph{initial condition}, since single-field inflation excites no first-order vector modes. The expansion only mildly reinforces it; it does not drive the limit. Cosmic time is therefore inherited rather than manufactured, and standard cosmology is recovered as a
gauge, not contradicted (\texttt{DTF\_localnow\_integrability.py}). The same division of labour as the arrow
(\S\ref{sec:rate} above): the framework locates the boundary condition and inherits the dynamics.

\emph{The numbers behind that sentence.} Three facts settle how much the expansion does and whether the
residual can be measured.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{The decay law is exact.} $\partial_\tau(Sa^2)=0$ for the vector metric perturbation, so
  $\omega\sim a^{-2}$.
\item \textbf{The expansion does little of the work.} In the radiation era $H\propto a^{-2}$ as well, so
  $\omega/H$ is \emph{frozen}. The approach to integrability begins only at matter--radiation equality, and
  has achieved a factor of $74$ since.
\item \textbf{The residual is unobservable.} At horizon scale $\Omega\sim H_0S/2$ gives
  $\lesssim10^{-11}$\,mas/yr, against Gravity Probe~B's $7.4$\,mas/yr; the tightest bound on a homogeneous
  vector mode, $(\sigma_V/H)_0<4.7\times10^{-11}$~\cite{Saadeh2016}, sits fifty orders above any realistic
  seed.
\end{itemize}
\noindent The residual is therefore \textbf{not a test}. DTF inherits general relativity's vector sector
equation for equation, so this is a consistency check the reframe passes rather than a place it sticks its
neck out (\texttt{DTF\_vorticity\_residual.py}).

Two boundaries: the holographic IR cutoff is the causal (future-event) horizon, not the Hubble sphere, so
$w\!\to\!-1$ is not forced (Hsu--Li~\cite{Hsu2004,Li2004}); and the rate is \emph{not} prior to the metric ---
clock-vs-neighbour is co-equal with the causal order ($c$) it is defined against, which is what
\emph{relational} means.

\subsection*{The engine: energy is a frequency, the lapse is the rate}

With the primitive fixed we read dynamics off, not add them. Two distinct ``rates'' meet here, and keeping them
apart is the whole point:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{The Compton frequency $\omega_C=mc^2/\hbar$} --- the particle's internal clock, \emph{per-species}
  and dimensionful (Hz). That energy is this frequency ($E=\hbar\omega_C$, with $E=mc^2$) is de~Broglie's
  clock~\cite{deBroglie1925}, measured in electron channeling~\cite{Catillon2008} and as a Compton-frequency
  clock~\cite{Lan2013}, and formalised by Dolce~\cite{Dolce2011} --- established prior art, not ours.
\item \textbf{The lapse $u=d\tau/dt$} --- the local proper-time rate, \emph{per-location}, geometric and
  dimensionless (the same for every species). This is the primitive.
\end{itemize}
These are \emph{not} equal. The new step, which de~Broglie and Dolce do not take, is to make $u$ the primitive and to notice that it does two jobs GR and QM keep apart: it \emph{redshifts} the clock (gravitational time
dilation is the variation of $u$) and it \emph{clocks} the phase, $\Phi=\omega_C\!\int u\,dt$. So redshift and phase are two readings of the one lapse (the shared $\Phi$), because both run through $u$, not because
$\omega_C$ is $u$. Whether the local $u$'s admit a global time function is then a separate Frobenius question
(\S\ref{sec:rate}), answered only in the vorticity-free limit, so cosmic time is emergent, not posited.

One drive then follows, matter falling toward slower clocks, under one rule: Fermat's principle on $\Phi$,
read at two sharpnesses (stationary $=$ classical mechanics, \S\ref{sec:equiv}; full sum $=$ quantum mechanics, \S\ref{sec:quantum}).

\setup{Everything below is \emph{forced} by the interval; energy is not a second ingredient.}
\begin{chainbox}
\known{S=-mc^2\!\int d\tau=-mc^2\!\int\!\sqrt{u^2-v^2/c^2}\,dt}{the full interval (not the rest form)}
\nodetext{$E=-\partial_t S=\gamma\,\hbar\omega_C\to\hbar\omega_C$ at rest. In the weak ($u=1+\phi/c^2$),
slow ($v\ll c$) limit $\mathcal L=-mc^2u+\tfrac12 mv^2$, whose Euler--Lagrange equation is
$\ddot{\mathbf x}=-c^2\nabla u=-\nabla\phi$. (The rest form $\int u\,dt$ alone gives $\nabla u=0$; the
heading term is required.)}
\dtfresult{E=\hbar\omega_C,\qquad \ddot{\mathbf x}=-c^2\nabla u}
{energy \emph{is} the internal frequency $\omega_C$; the drive falls down the gradient of the lapse $u$}
\end{chainbox}
\begin{tcolorbox}[keybox]
\textbf{One drive, one rule, two sharpnesses.}\ The drive is $\ddot{\mathbf x}=-c^2\nabla u$; the rule is
Fermat on the accumulated clock reading
$\Phi=S/\hbar=-\omega_C\!\int d\tau=-\omega_C\!\int\!\sqrt{u^2-v^2/c^2}\,dt$ --- the \emph{one} variational
object used throughout (its rest reduction $\int u\,dt$ drops the heading $v^2$ term and gives $\nabla u=0$,
so the full interval is required; \S\ref{sec:quantum} varies this same $\Phi$). Stationary path $\Rightarrow$ gravity and
inertia (\S\ref{sec:equiv}); full sum $\Rightarrow$ quantum mechanics (\S\ref{sec:quantum}). The three constants are one tick seen three ways:
$c$ (period $\to$ length, Row $1\!\to\!2$), $mc^2=\hbar\omega_C$ (rate in energy units, Row 1),
$\hbar$ (the localisation tax $\lambda_C=\hbar/mc$, Row 2).
\end{tcolorbox}
\statusline{Same $\Phi$ throughout (\texttt{DTF\_phase\_unification.py}). Two rates, not conflated: $\omega_C$
is the particle's Compton tick, $u$ the gravitational rate that redshifts it ($d\tau=u\,dt$ at rest) --- so
``one primitive'' means one \emph{kind} of rate, not one number (masses stay inputs, Appendix~E).}

\section{Entropy and the arrow of time}\label{sec:arrow}
\worksin{Row 0 $\to$ Row 1 (the timeless state $\to$ the clock's tick-count)}


Entropy here means the ordinary microstate count,
\[ S=\ln N , \]
the logarithm of the number of distinguishable states a system has reached, and the paper uses no other. That
the thermal entropy of a bath follows from this count is textbook and is not re-derived; the question taken up
here is the one before it. The rate primitive answers a question statistical mechanics takes as already settled: \emph{what makes states distinguishable at all.} Temperature cannot be that
answer, because it is \emph{defined} from the entropy ($1/T=\partial S/\partial E$) and so presupposes the
count. The primitive can be: $N$ is the number of ticks the clock has resolved since the clocks first emerged in sync (the Row~0$\to$1 transition), and the
same $N$ is read three ways,
\[
E=N\hbar\omega\ \ (\text{linearly}),\qquad \Phi=2\pi N\ \ (\text{as phase}),\qquad S=\ln N\ \ (\text{as a log}),
\]
with temperature nowhere among them.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2.5pt,topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Orientation is derived; the low-entropy start is not.} Proper time $\tau=\int u\,dt$ only grows, since $u>0$, so each clock has a native orientation. That is not yet the thermodynamic arrow. The arrow needs the clocks to begin in sync: $N=1$, so $S=0$ exactly, at the Row~0$\to$1 transition. That is the ``past hypothesis''~\cite{Albert2000} that a time-symmetric dynamics must be handed, restated as the clocks' common origin. The framework locates it and does not supply a reason for it.
\item \textbf{Ticks are what become distinguishable.} A spread $\sigma_u$ in the local rates desynchronises
  phases, resolving $N\simeq\omega\,\sigma_u t$ states, so $S=\ln N$ grows as $\ln t$. The simulation checks the \emph{bookkeeping}, not the arrow: the fit $S=a\ln t+b$ returns $a=0.982$ against the
  predicted $1$, and doubling \emph{either} $\omega$ or $\sigma_u$ shifts $S$ by $0.693$ against $\ln2=0.6931$
  --- confirming that both enter multiplicatively \emph{inside} $N$ and never additively in $S$. Since
  $N\propto\omega\sigma_u t$ was put in, this is a consistency check on a definition, not evidence for it.
\item \textbf{Growth is entanglement, not loss.} Row 0 stays globally pure ($S_{\rm global}=0$ at all times);
  what rises is a subsystem's share, linearly in the tick count (slope $0.66$ in a transverse-field Ising
  quench). The arrow is the Row-0 ledger distributing correlations, which is also why nothing is destroyed at a
  black-hole core (\S\ref{sec:consistency}). Consistent with experiment: in an isolated six-atom lattice the global state stays pure
  while subsystems reach the values a thermometer would report~\cite{Kaufman2016} --- an illustration of the
  reading, not a test of it.
\item \textbf{Counting before heating is standard.} Landauer's erasure cost $k_BT\ln2$ already separates the
  count $\ln2$ from its price $k_BT$~\cite{Landauer1961,Berut2012}; a count survives at
  $T\to0$~\cite{Pauling1935}; the Kolmogorov--Sinai rate counts \emph{per unit time} with no temperature in
  it~\cite{Pesin1977}. None of this is new. The one new step is to identify $N$ with the clock.

\section{The quantum rate field}\label{sec:quantum}
\worksin{Row 1 (single particle); Row 0 (entanglement); the tax is Row 2 ($\hbar$)}

This section adds no equation to quantum mechanics: it is a \emph{compatibility} reading, importing Madelung,
Fisher-uniqueness, Bohmian guidance, and Born-as-typicality wholesale and asking only what the rate primitive
reidentifies. Its one net-new element is that the phase is a clock reading, which gives Wallstrom's condition a
physical reason (now shown dynamically stable, \S\ref{sec:quantum}); everything else is standard, relabelled.

The Fermat principle at its \emph{other} sharpness is quantum mechanics, and the wave is not abstract. Write
\[ \psi=\sqrt{\rho}\;e^{i\Phi},\qquad \Phi=S/\hbar . \]
Both symbols already have meanings from \S\ref{sec:rate}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2.5pt,topsep=3pt]
\item $\Phi$ is the accumulated clock reading.
\item $\rho$ is the density of definite clock-rate; a particle is a localized excitation of it above the
  Row-0 floor.
\end{itemize}
\noindent So the wavefunction \emph{is} the rate field rather than an abstraction over it.

\emph{One honest cost.} That literal reading holds for one particle in ordinary space. For $N$ particles the
guiding wave lives on the $3N$-dimensional configuration space, so the ontology carries $O(N^2)$ correlational
structure where a naive space field would carry $O(N)$. Every realist quantum theory pays this, and it is why
entanglement is booked on Row~0 rather than as a field in space.

\medskip
\noindent Substituted into Schr\"odinger's equation, the polar form splits \emph{exactly}~\cite{Madelung1927}:
\[
\underbrace{\partial_t\rho+\nabla\!\cdot\!\Big(\rho\,\frac{\nabla S}{m}\Big)=0}_{\text{continuity}}
\qquad\qquad
\underbrace{\partial_t S+\frac{(\nabla S)^2}{2m}+V+Q=0}_{\text{the clock-action, plus one term}}
\]
and that one extra term is the localization tax,
\[ Q=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\nabla^2\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho}
    =\frac{\delta}{\delta\rho}\int\frac{\hbar^2}{8m}\frac{(\nabla\rho)^2}{\rho}\,d^3x , \]
the variation of the Fisher functional~\cite{Reginatto1998,Frieden1989}.

\noindent DTF adds no term. It reads the two already there, and three results follow.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{The tax is forced, not chosen.} Among local terms with at most two derivatives, the Fisher form
  is the \emph{unique} one that keeps the $\psi$-equation linear (\texttt{DTF\_o9\_fisher\_unique.py}). Why
  quantum mechanics is linear is not addressed.
\item \textbf{Born is not a separate axiom.} The current $j=\rho\nabla S/m$ makes $|\psi|^2$ equivariant, so
  Born frequencies are a typicality assumption~\cite{DGZ1992,Valentini1991} rather than a theorem, and objects
  stay definite under $v=\nabla S/m$~\cite{Bohm1952}. One residual remains: which outcome a lone detection
  selects (O1).
\item \textbf{Discreteness is topological.} A clock reading is $S^1$-valued, so $\psi$ is single-valued with
  integer circulation around nodes. This gives Wallstrom's condition~\cite{Wallstrom1994} a physical reason,
  and the winding stays exactly integer through moving nodes (\texttt{DTF\_winding\_dynamics.py}).
  (Fermionic $\psi\to-\psi$ is spin structure, not a counterexample; single-valuedness is an input here.)
\end{itemize}

\emph{Entanglement.} It needs no cosmic ``now''. A joint state's correlations live in the \emph{timeless} Row-0 ledger,
not on a slice: each outcome is definite \emph{locally}, and the correlation appears only when results are
brought together. Nothing orders spacelike-separated outcomes, so the account is frame-neutral (contrast
relativistic pilot-wave theories~\cite{DGMZ1999,Tumulka2006}, which must postulate a foliation). The ledger is timeless rather than a past hidden variable, so it evades Bell's factorisation and is compatible with the quantum bound. The CHSH operator obeys

(the standard Tsirelson bound, which QM saturates; the classical $2$ is the commuting case;

\[ P(\alpha,\beta\mid x,y)=\big|\langle\alpha\beta\mid\pi(x,y)\,\Omega\rangle\big|^2 ,

The second equation is completeness of the reads on each handle, which here is Born as the conserved

\[ \partial_y\!\sum_\beta P(\alpha,\beta\mid x,y)=0 \]
while the joint keeps both settings and Bell remains violable.

\item \textbf{What this does not do.} It does not \emph{derive} the restriction. The same computation returns

\item \textbf{What it does do.} It gives a \emph{reason to expect} locality of reads --- space is an output, so

\emph{The information paradox dissolves the same way.} There is no singularity to destroy information: infalling
matter de-emerges from Row~1 to the \emph{timeless} Row-0 ledger --- the same ledger that already holds
entanglement correlations (\S\ref{sec:quantum}). The correlations are not erased, only removed from Row-1 time; Hawking
radiation (a Row-2, statistical property) carries off energy, while the information sits in Row~0 and in the
stable horizonless remnant. It appears thermal only because its entanglement partner de-emerged to Row~0 rather
than being destroyed --- trace out that partner and one gets thermality, keep it and the total state is pure.
This evades the usual species/entropy objection to remnants: the information is \emph{not} in the remnant's
local degrees of freedom (whose capacity is set by its Planck mass) but in Row~0, whose capacity is not.
Loss of unitarity was an artifact of assuming a singular endpoint in Row-1 time; with none, there is nothing to
lose. (Retention class, at the conjecture level of the core; no Page-curve or evaporation dynamics are claimed.)

\emph{(ii) MOND scale --- $a_0$ grounded.} MOND's standing complaint is that its acceleration $a_0$ is a
\emph{free measured constant}: the algorithm is untethered. Local-now supplies a tether, from Row-1 geometry
alone (no temperature). A body's self-field reach $c^2/a$ can cohere with the cosmic clock out to one full cycle, the horizon
circumference $2\pi R_H=2\pi c/H_0$. Dynamics therefore depart from Newton at $a_0=c^2/2\pi R_H=cH_0/2\pi$
(Appendix~B), where the $2\pi$ is the clock's radians-per-cycle rather than a thermal factor. The relevant
horizon is the late de~Sitter (future-event) horizon, the same one \S\ref{sec:rate} requires for $w\!\to\!-1$. So $a_0$ is
\emph{constant} at $\sim cH_0$ today rather than tracking $H(z)$, which avoids tension with high-$z$ disks. The Milgrom
interpolation
\[ \mu=\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+a_0^2}} \]
then follows from a two-horizon quadrature (Rindler $\kappa_a=a$, de~Sitter
$cH_0$)~\cite{DeserLevin1997,Milgrom1983} \emph{as an assignment, not a theorem}. The modified-\emph{inertia}
route that would distinguish DTF from MOND is \emph{falsified} (stellar-stream apsides); orbits are MOND's
modified-\emph{gravity} (AQUAL) shadow, with no new galactic prediction. Appendix~B carries the grade and the caveats. The chief one: $cH_0$, $cH_0/2\pi$ and $cH_0/6$ all sit inside
the SPARC band ($0.205$ against $0.201$ dex~\cite{Lelli2016}), so the fit alone does not pin the coefficient.

\emph{(iii) Cosmological origin --- time's emergence.} The Big Bang is the \emph{onset of a coherent now} (the
Row-0$\to$Row-1 transition at cosmic scale), the time-reverse of the core (i): coherence sets in at the Planck
floor $N=1$ (finite, not $a=0$), so there is no initial singularity and no ``before,'' and the horizon problem
does not arise --- uniformity is inherited from the timeless Row-0 floor, not established across a global slice.
Whether the emergence also drives inflation is \emph{left open} (it would need a transiently incomplete
invariance, $\pi_u\neq0$; O4).

Late times: the redshift is unchanged ($1+z=a_{\rm obs}/a_{\rm emit}$; homogeneous $u(t)$ is gauge), and the
one finite causal horizon $L=c/H_0$ fixes both dark scales,
\[ \Omega_\Lambda=d^2\sim O(1), \qquad a_0\sim cH_0, \qquad \rho_\Lambda/\rho_P\sim(\ell_P/L)^2\sim10^{-122} . \]
All at conjecture level: the observed $\Omega_\Lambda=0.68$ \emph{would require} $d\approx0.82$ (not forced),
and $w=-1$ needs the future event horizon (Hsu--Li~\cite{Hsu2004,Li2004}). Scripts:
\texttt{DTF\_cosmology\_timeline}, \texttt{DTF\_de\_horizon}, \texttt{DTF\_darksectors\_unified}.

\section{Limitations and open problems}\label{sec:open}
\noindent These open problems are of a particular kind, and the kind follows from the choice this paper
makes. Adding no equation to general relativity or quantum mechanics means the debts owed here are
arguments rather than measurements; a framework that changed predictions would owe experiments instead,
and should be judged by them. Several results above are supplied with motivated resolutions rather than
theorems, and are listed here at that strength. On single-time statistics and on any complete-plane flux, DTF is \emph{provably}
identical to quantum mechanics (\S\ref{sec:quantum}), so no experiment of that kind can discriminate; the framework's
gravitational exposure is the black-hole remnant (\S\ref{sec:consistency}), and within quantum mechanics no channel survives
at all (\S\ref{sec:quantum}): the divergence-free freedom that could have carried one is not sourced by the tax.
Two quantities that might be expected to serve as discriminators do not: monotonic mean curvature
($\dot H\le0$) requires a preferred slice this framework does not have, and the decaying-vorticity residual
is unobservable by fifty orders of magnitude (\S\ref{sec:rate}).\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.9em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt,label=\textbf{O\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Outcome selection.} Guidance $v=\nabla S/m$ plus an actual beable makes $|\psi|^2$ the typicality
measure over the beable's unknown initial value; the residual --- \emph{why this initial configuration} --- is
the typicality floor every no-collapse theory shares, not a debt peculiar to DTF (\S\ref{sec:quantum}).
\item \textbf{Black-hole core coefficient.} The core \emph{location} is coefficient-free ($N=L_{\rm
curv}/\ell_P=1$, Planck curvature, for any $p\in[\tfrac12,1]$); open is the $O(1)$ coefficient of
$\delta u/u\sim(\ell_P/L)^p$ --- the precise shell radius --- needing a Row-0 foam propagator (\S\ref{sec:consistency}).
\item \textbf{Does the projection generate the tensor structure?} No-signalling follows from
handle-local reads plus completeness (\S\ref{sec:quantum}), and the row ordering explains why reads should be
handle-local --- but the two-handle tensor structure is assumed, not built. Whether the
Row-0$\to$Row-2 projection \emph{generates} that structure or presupposes it decides whether
no-signalling is derived or only motivated here.
\item \textbf{Transient inflation.} If the Row-0$\to$Row-1 emergence drives inflation (\S\ref{sec:consistency}), it needs a
transiently incomplete reparametrization invariance ($\pi_u\neq0$); an action for that phase and any CMB
observables ($n_s,r$) are unshown (\S\ref{sec:consistency}).
\item \textbf{The driver of clock desync.} The arrow follows from a spread $\sigma_u$ in local rates
(\S\ref{sec:rate}, App.~G), but what \emph{grows} $\sigma_u$ from the Row-0 synced state is gravitational clumping ---
argued from the sign of the instability, not simulated. The entropy results are conditional on it.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Technical Appendix}

\noindent\textbf{A.\ \ Two polarisations and $\alpha_1=\alpha_2=0$ (\S\ref{sec:gravity}).}\ \
Count in ADM variables~\cite{ADM1962}: the spatial metric $\gamma_{ij}$ and conjugate $\pi^{ij}$ give a
12-dimensional phase space per point, with four first-class constraints (one Hamiltonian, three momentum), each
removing two dimensions:
\[ N_{\rm dof}=\tfrac12\big(12-2\times4\big)=2 \qquad (\text{the transverse-traceless pair } h_{ij}^{TT}), \]
exactly the GR count, since the dynamics are Einstein's. The lapse and shift are multipliers; the lapse has
$\pi_u\equiv0$ (\S\ref{sec:rate}), so it contributes no scalar (breathing) mode and no vector clock-current. That absence
forces the preferred-frame parameters to vanish --- the gravitomagnetic $g_{0i}$ from boosting the static
solution and from solving for a moving source agree exactly:
\[ \Delta g_{0i}=0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \alpha_1=\alpha_2=0 . \]
Scripts: \texttt{DTF\_radiative\_content\_constructive.py}, \texttt{DTF\_alpha1\_explicit\_1PN.py}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{B.\ \ The interpolation function and $a_0$ (\S\ref{sec:consistency}), a Row-1 derivation.}\ \
The MOND scale is derived here from Row-1 quantities only --- causal-horizon geometry and the rate primitive
--- with \emph{no} appeal to temperature, which is a Row-2 (statistical) object and may not enter a derivation
of a Row-1 scale.

\emph{The scale.} The primitive is a clock, and a clock is defined by cycles: one cycle is $2\pi$ of phase. The
cosmic causal horizon has radius $R_H=c/H_0$, so one full cycle of the horizon-scale clock spans its
circumference $2\pi R_H$. A body's self-field reach is the Rindler length $L_a=c^2/a$. Coherence with the cosmic
clock is lost once the reach exceeds one cosmic cycle, $L_a=2\pi R_H$, giving
\[
a_0=\frac{c^2}{2\pi R_H}=\frac{cH_0}{2\pi}=1.08\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}},
\]
against Milgrom's measured $1.20\times10^{-10}$ (a $\sim10\%$ match under a $\sim20\%$ systematic). The $2\pi$
is the fundamental clock's radians-per-cycle times the horizon circumference --- both Row-1 --- \emph{not} a
thermal $2\pi$; a temperature route (Unruh $\propto a/2\pi$ against de~Sitter $\propto H_0/2\pi$) instead
\emph{cancels} the $2\pi$ and gives $a_0=cH_0$, which is why DTF does not take it.

\emph{The interpolation.} A body's acceleration and the cosmic horizon define two \emph{surface gravities}
$\kappa_a=a$ and $\kappa_{\rm dS}=cH_0=a_0$ --- geometric, Row-1. They add in quadrature~\cite{DeserLevin1997},
\[
\kappa_{\rm eff}=\sqrt{a^2+a_0^2},
\]
and assigning the acceleration-attributable share $\mu=\kappa_a/\kappa_{\rm eff}=a/\sqrt{a^2+a_0^2}$ reproduces
Milgrom's form. This projection is an \emph{assignment}, not a theorem, and it uses only surface gravities ---
geometry, not temperature.

\emph{Fit and caveats.} At fixed $a_0=cH_0/2\pi$ the $175$-galaxy SPARC scatter is $0.205$ dex, versus $0.201$
at best-fit $a_0$ --- a $2\%$ degradation, so ``zero free parameters'' is defensible, though the fit alone
cannot distinguish $2\pi$ from a nearby $O(1)$, and the one residual is firming that the reach saturates at
\emph{exactly} one cycle (not a half or two). Orbits precess as MOND and dark matter do (Orphan~\cite{Koposov2019},
Sagittarius~\cite{Vasiliev2021}); the modified-\emph{inertia} route that would have distinguished DTF is
falsified, so DTF reproduces MOND's modified-\emph{gravity} (AQUAL) shadow. This is distinct from
thermodynamic entropic-gravity routes~\cite{Jacobson1995,Verlinde2011}, which pass through Row-2 temperature.
Scripts: \texttt{DTF\_mond\_quadrature\_zeta.py}, \texttt{DTF\_sparc\_fixed\_a0.py},
\texttt{DTF\_stream\_apsidal.py}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{C.\ \ Schr\"odinger from the localization tax (\S\ref{sec:quantum}).}\ \
In polar form $\psi=\sqrt\rho\,e^{iS/\hbar}$ the Schr\"odinger equation
\[ i\hbar\,\partial_t\psi=-\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi+V\psi \]
separates \emph{exactly}~\cite{Madelung1927} into an imaginary part (continuity, conservation of the rate
density) and a real part (the quantum Hamilton--Jacobi equation):
\[ \partial_t\rho+\nabla\!\cdot\!\big(\rho\,\tfrac{\nabla S}{m}\big)=0 , \qquad
   \partial_t S+\frac{(\nabla S)^2}{2m}+V+Q=0 , \qquad
   Q=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\nabla^2\!\sqrt\rho}{\sqrt\rho} . \]
The first two terms of the real part are \S\ref{sec:rate}'s classical clock-action; the sole non-classical term is $Q$,
and it is the Euler--Lagrange variation of the localization-tax (Fisher/von Weizs\"acker) functional,
\[
Q=\frac{\delta}{\delta\rho}\int\frac{\hbar^2}{8m}\frac{(\nabla\rho)^2}{\rho}\,d^3x
\qquad\text{(Reginatto~\cite{Reginatto1998})},
\]
carrying the same $\hbar$ the ledger names in $\lambda_C=\hbar/mc$. Both identities are verified symbolically
(\texttt{DTF\_localization\_tax.py}). Born's measure is not a second postulate: the native current
$j=\rho\nabla S/m$ makes $\rho=|\psi|^2$ the unique equivariant density, read out by all-or-nothing detection.
\emph{An operational transit speed.} Guidance is the current $v=\nabla S/m$ (Born-equivariant). In a forbidden
region $\psi\sim e^{-\kappa x}$ is real, so $\nabla S\to0$ (beable at rest), yet the tax is nonzero, giving an
\emph{operational} speed
\[ Q=-\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\kappa^2 , \qquad v_{\rm op}=\sqrt{2|Q|/m}=\hbar\kappa/m , \]
which matches the $10^3$--$10^4\,$km\,s$^{-1}$ magnitudes of Sharoglazova \emph{et al.}~\cite{Sharoglazova2025}
at photonic mass (\texttt{DTF\_evanescent\_speed.py}). Two caveats: the $O(1)$ coefficient is not pinned by the
data (as for $a_0$); and $v_{\rm op}$ is \emph{not} a beable velocity --- the challenge to Bohmian mechanics
has been rebutted~\cite{DrezetLazaroviciNabet2026,Nikolic2025}, the authors maintaining their
position~\cite{SharoglazovaReply2025}.

\noindent\textbf{D.\ \ Electromagnetism on the clock-phase (compatibility, \S\ref{sec:quantum}).}\ \
Only phase differences are physical, so the phase origin may be reset locally, $\psi\to e^{i\alpha(x)}\psi$; a
connection restores covariance,
\[ A_\mu\to A_\mu+\tfrac{\hbar c}{q}\partial_\mu\alpha, \qquad D_\mu=\partial_\mu-\tfrac{iq}{\hbar c}A_\mu . \]
Then $F_{\mu\nu}$ is gauge-invariant, the homogeneous Maxwell pair holds identically, varying
$-\tfrac14 F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}-J^\mu A_\mu$ gives the source pair, a mass term is forbidden (massless photon),
and minimal coupling adds the Lorentz force to the \S\ref{sec:rate} drive:
\[ m\ddot{\mathbf x}=-mc^2\nabla u+q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B) \]
(\texttt{DTF\_em\_from\_phase.py}). This is the standard $U(1)$ construction under the phase${}={}$clock-reading
identification --- a \emph{consistency check}, not a derivation: DTF fixes neither $\alpha$ nor the charge
assignments. Two points make it Row-native, not bolted on:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Local reset is not an extra postulate.} A global phase convention is the global reference \S\ref{sec:rate}
  forgoes; with none, the origin is fixed only locally. A globally single-valued phase exists iff $F\equiv0$,
  so denying a global referent is what admits a radiating $F\neq0$ (\texttt{DTF\_em\_clockphase\_forced.py}).
\item \textbf{Clock-phase, not the frame.} Gauging the frame's internal $SO(3)$ instead gives three
  Planck-mass vectors and no photon (the tetrad VEV is a full-rank order parameter,
  \texttt{DTF\_em\_frame\_gauge.py}); gravity (frame) and electromagnetism (frame-orthogonal $U(1)$) exhaust
  the long-range content from the triad VEV (\texttt{DTF\_force\_census.py}).
\end{itemize}
Charge quantisation would rest on the single-valuedness discussed in \S\ref{sec:quantum}, so none is asserted.

\noindent\textbf{G.\ \ The entropy ledger (\S\ref{sec:rate}): one entropy, one count.}\ \
Throughout this appendix the entropy is $S=\ln N$, the ordinary logarithm of a count of distinguishable
states. No second quantity is introduced. What is derived is $N$ --- how many states the clock has resolved ---
and every number below is obtained without temperature; heat appears in exactly one paragraph, as an
alternative way of reaching a large $N$.

\emph{One count, three readings.} With $N$ the accumulated distinguishable ticks,
\[
E=N\hbar\omega,\qquad \Phi=2\pi N,\qquad S=\ln N ,
\]
so $S=\ln(E/\hbar\omega)$: entropy and energy are not independent bookkeeping. At the start of Row~1, $N=1$ and $S=0$.

\emph{The desync law, and what it does not do.} Give the local rates a spread $\sigma_u$ about the mean.
Phases separate as $\phi_i=\omega u_i t$, so the occupied phase width --- the tick count --- is
$N\simeq\omega\sigma_u t$ and $S=\ln N=\ln(\omega\sigma_u t)$. Numerically ($4\times10^{5}$ clocks, $4096$
phase cells, unsaturated window): the fit $S=a\ln t+b$ gives $a=0.982$ against the predicted $1$; doubling
$\sigma_u$ at fixed $t$ shifts $S$ by $0.6936$ and doubling $\omega$ by $0.6924$, against $\ln2=0.6931$ ---
both to better than $0.1\%$. Two disclaimers, since this is the section's most quotable number. First, the
input was $N\propto\omega\sigma_u t$, so the fit verifies that the code implements the definition and that
$\omega,\sigma_u$ act multiplicatively inside $N$; it is not independent evidence that entropy grows.
Second, the simulated quantity is $-\sum p\ln p$, which sits $0.70$--$0.79$ nats below $\ln(\text{occupied
cells})$ for a Gaussian spread --- an additive offset, not a change in the law --- so the honest statement is
$S=\ln N+O(1)$, with $\ln N$ the equal-weight ceiling. A discrete, commensurate set of rates instead recurs
(Poincar\'e; $S$ oscillates in $[0,1.79]$): the law is reversible, the monotone arrow needs many degrees of
freedom plus the $N=1$ start, and DTF locates the start, at the Row~0$\to$1 transition, without deriving it; the mixing is inherited.

\emph{Purity is preserved.} In a transverse-field Ising quench the global state stays pure
($S_{\rm global}=0$ to machine precision at all times) while a subsystem's entanglement entropy grows linearly
in the tick count (slope $0.663$) before saturating at $\ln(\dim)$. The Row-1 arrow is therefore the
\emph{redistribution} of a conserved Row-0 correlation budget, which is why nothing is destroyed at a
black-hole core (\S\ref{sec:consistency}).

\emph{The rate bound.} Margolus--Levitin~\cite{MargolusLevitin1998} caps the tick rate at
$dN/d\tau\le 2E/\pi\hbar$; applied to the observable universe this returns Lloyd's $N\sim10^{120}$
ticks~\cite{Lloyd2002}. The arrow's maximum speed \emph{is} the energy --- the same identity as $E=N\hbar\omega$
differentiated.

\emph{Reaching a large count by heating instead.} Put the oscillator in a bath, so occupation rather than
desync resolves the states. With the zero-point half-tick included the count is
$N_{\rm th}=\tfrac12+1/(e^{\hbar\omega/k_BT}-1)$, and the large-$N$ form of the same $S=\ln N$ is
$\ln(eN_{\rm th})$. Against the exact oscillator entropy $S=(1{+}n)\ln(1{+}n)-n\ln n$ with
$n=1/(e^{\hbar\omega/k_BT}-1)$:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrrr@{}}
\toprule
$k_BT/\hbar\omega$ & $N_{\rm th}$ & exact $S$ & $\ln(eN_{\rm th})$\\\midrule
$0.5$ & $0.657$ & $0.458$ & $0.579$\\
$1$ & $1.082$ & $1.041$ & $1.079$\\
$5$ & $5.017$ & $2.611$ & $2.613$\\
$50$ & $50.00$ & $4.912$ & $4.912$\\
$500$ & $500.0$ & $7.215$ & $7.215$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent The agreement is \emph{asymptotic}: four digits once $N_{\rm th}\gtrsim5$, but a $26\%$ error at
$N_{\rm th}<1$, where a count-of-states reading is not valid anyway. (Dropping the zero-point half-tick makes
$\ln(eN)$ negative at low $T$, which is the diagnostic that the half belongs in the count.) The point is not
numerical precision but that nothing about the \emph{entropy} changed --- only what resolved the states. That
ordering is not ours to choose: temperature is \emph{defined} from entropy ($1/T=\partial S/\partial E$), and
Landauer's $k_BT\ln2$ likewise separates a count $\ln2$ from a price $k_BT$~\cite{Landauer1961,Berut2012}. A
count without a bath is routine physics --- it survives at $T\to0$~\cite{Pauling1935}, it is what is measured
in a globally pure state~\cite{Kaufman2016}, and the Kolmogorov--Sinai rate counts per unit time with no
temperature in it~\cite{Pesin1977}. DTF adds no entropy; it identifies $N$ with the clock.

\emph{Which share dominates.} Measured budgets~\cite{EganLineweaver2010} put the black-hole share of the
observable universe at $3.1\times10^{104}k_B$ against the microwave background's $\sim\!10^{88}k_B$, with the
cosmic event horizon at $2.6\times10^{122}k_B$; our coarse estimate reproduces these to an order of magnitude.
Radiation holds a negligible fraction of $N$, and the dominant share --- carried by clumping, i.e.\ by
departures of $u$ from uniformity --- is near its minimum when $u$ is uniform. A hot but smooth start is
therefore a \emph{small} $N$, which is Penrose's point~\cite{Penrose1979} restated as a question of shares
rather than an added postulate. Honest limit: how to count the clumping share has no agreed formula, and
black-hole entropy is doing duty as the proxy.
